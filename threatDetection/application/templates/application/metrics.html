Cybersecurity Evaluation Principles & Mathematical Formulations

In email security operations, high accuracy alone does not guarantee model reliability. An effective phishing detection system must evaluate trade-offs between two failure modes:

False Negative (FN = 26 • 1.99%): A malicious phishing email is mistakenly classified as safe. This represents a critical operational breach risk where employees may be compromised. This is why our system integrates Llama 3.2 neural reasoning as a secondary defense layer.
False Positive (FP = 44 • 2.00%): A legitimate email is mistakenly classified as phishing. This causes operational friction and alert fatigue. Our 98.00% specificity ensures minimal false alarms.
